Notice board

The summer fete will be held on the last Saturday of July on the green. Stalls are free for residents. Volunteers are needed for the tombola, the tea tent and the very serious business of the dog show.

Lost property from the village hall is displayed in the cupboard by the kitchen, and will be donated to the charity shop at the end of the month.

The bus shelter has been repainted. Please do not lean on it for a few days, however tempting.
