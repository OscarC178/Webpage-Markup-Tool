Highlighter and notes for web pages and PDFs. Highlight text in five colours, underline key phrases and add notes beside what you read. Everything is saved automatically and is there when you come back.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Highlight and underline
Select any text and pick a colour. Highlights stay readable on dark websites, and they never move the page around.

Add notes to any passage
Attach a note to the text you select. Notes sit in the margin beside the passage, so you can see your thinking at a glance.

Annotate PDFs
Click the extension's icon on a PDF and choose "Mark up this PDF". Highlight, underline and add notes just as you do on a web page. Your marks stay with the PDF, even if you open it again from a different link or from your computer.

Come back to your work
Your highlights and notes reappear every time you revisit a page, even when the site has changed its layout. If a passage has been removed, the mark and its note are kept and listed for you.

Export your highlights and notes
Copy everything as Markdown, download it as a file, or print and save the page as a PDF with your highlights in place and your notes listed at the end.

Private by design
Your highlights and notes are saved only on your own computer. Nothing is collected, and there is no account to create.

Keyboard shortcuts
Alt+Shift+H to mark the selected text. Alt+Shift+N to jump to the next note.

WHO IT IS FOR

Students: turn online articles, textbooks and lecture PDFs into study notes for revision and exams.

Researchers: collect quotes, data and sources from across the web and export them into one document.

Writers and professionals: annotate articles and reports, and keep the passages you need for your own work.

Curious readers: build a personal library of the things worth remembering.

GOOD TO KNOW

Marking up PDFs needs Chrome 147 or newer; web pages work on older versions. Scanned PDFs (pictures of pages with no text) can be read and printed, but not marked up. To mark up PDFs saved on your computer, use "Open a file…" in the PDF viewer, or switch on "Allow access to file URLs" for the extension in chrome://extensions.
