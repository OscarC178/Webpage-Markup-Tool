Frequently asked

Can I park at the village hall?

Yes. There is space for about twelve cars behind the hall, and a further six on the green when it has not been raining. Please leave the disabled bay free, and be considerate of the playgroup on Wednesday mornings.

How do I report a pothole?

Use the form above or call the clerk. Include the road name and, if you can, a nearby house number. Photographs are welcome but not required.
