Incident notes

Monday 03:12. A certificate on the image proxy expired without warning, and for about eleven minutes the product pages loaded without pictures. Nobody was woken, because the alert was routed to a mailbox that nobody has read since the spring reorganisation. The routing has since been corrected.

Tuesday 14:40. A deployment to the search service doubled median latency for roughly half an hour. The cause was an index that had been rebuilt with the wrong analyser, so every query for a plural noun was also searching for the singular. It was fast to fix and slow to understand.

Wednesday 09:05. The weekly export to finance failed because a column called region had been renamed to area by someone who was, by all accounts, having a perfectly reasonable morning. The export was re-run by hand.

Thursday 22:18. A burst of traffic from a link on a gardening forum briefly tripled the number of visitors per minute. The system coped. The gardeners, we are told, were looking for a recipe for rhubarb gin.

Friday 11:30. No incidents. The team used the quiet to delete forty-one dashboards that nobody could remember creating, and to write the first draft of a runbook for expired certificates. It is, for now, three lines long, and the first line is "check the date".

Saturday 06:00. A scheduled backup ran for four minutes longer than usual and then finished. Nobody has been able to say why, and nobody is willing to say that it was a coincidence.
