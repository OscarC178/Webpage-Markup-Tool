A tall, slim reading lamp with a pleated linen shade and a base turned from solid ash. It gives a warm, even light that is kind to tired eyes, and the weighted foot means it will stay put even when a cat disagrees with its position.

Made in small batches in a workshop that, like this shop, is entirely imaginary. Dispatched within three working days. Free returns for thirty days, no questions, only a polite enquiry about what went wrong.

The Ashdown began as a sketch on the back of a seed packet. The designer wanted a lamp that would sit beside an armchair and ask for nothing: no fiddly dimmer, no cold white glare, no cable that insists on tangling itself around a slipper. The result is a single warm setting, a switch you can find in the dark, and a shade whose pleats are folded by hand, one at a time, by someone with remarkable patience.

The ash is sourced from managed woodland, finished with a plant-based oil, and left deliberately a little rough so that it ages with the room rather than against it.
